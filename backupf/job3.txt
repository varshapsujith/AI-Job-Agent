We are looking for a Business Analyst & Process Improvement Specialist to analyze business processes, identify operational gaps, gather business requirements, and drive process improvements across the organization.

The ideal candidate will have strong analytical and problem-solving skills, experience in business process analysis, requirements gathering, process mapping, stakeholder management, and data-driven decision-making, with the ability to translate business needs into practical solutions.

Key Responsibilities

Analyze existing business processes and identify gaps, bottlenecks, inefficiencies, and improvement opportunities.

Conduct AS-IS and TO-BE process analysis and develop optimized workflows.
Gather, analyze, document, and validate business requirements from stakeholders.
Conduct stakeholder interviews, workshops, discussions, and requirement-gathering sessions.
Prepare and maintain BRDs, FRDs/FSDs, SRS, user stories, process flows, use cases, SOPs, and requirement documentation.
Perform gap analysis, impact analysis, root cause analysis, and process analysis.
Develop process maps, workflows, flowcharts, and other visual documentation.
Work closely with business, operations, technology, and other cross-functional teams to understand business challenges and implement solutions.
Identify opportunities for process standardization, automation, cost reduction, time reduction, and productivity improvement.
Define and monitor KPIs and performance metrics to measure process effectiveness.
Analyze business and operational data to identify trends, issues, and opportunities for improvement.
Prepare MIS reports, dashboards, management reports, and business insights as required.
Support implementation of new processes, systems, and business solutions.
Track process changes and measure the impact of implemented improvements.
Identify potential risks and dependencies associated with proposed process changes.
Support User Acceptance Testing (UAT), implementation, and post-implementation review where required.
Prepare SOPs and process documentation to ensure consistent adoption of improved processes.
Communicate findings, recommendations, and improvement plans to management and relevant stakeholders.

Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Business Analytics, Finance, Computer Science, Engineering, Operations, or a related field.
2–5 years of experience in Business Analysis, Process Improvement, Business Operations, Consulting, or a related role.
Strong hands-on experience in business process analysis and improvement.
Experience in requirements gathering and stakeholder management.
Strong understanding of AS-IS / TO-BE process mapping and gap analysis.
Experience creating process flows, workflows, SOPs, BRDs, FRDs/FSDs, SRS, or user stories.

Strong communication and presentation skills.
Ability to work independently as well as collaborate with cross-functional teams.
Strong attention to detail with a structured and data-driven approach.
